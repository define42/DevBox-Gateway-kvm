- skip_dct_quant_amd64.s: exact 8-byte pixel loads, same AVX2 DCT arithmetic, in-register unsigned-saturating quantization and signed maxima semantics from existing quantFour4x4MaxAVX2, 128-byte coefficient output + 8-byte maxima.
- skip_dct_quant_generic.go: returns unhandled; original implementation on other architectures.

TEST SCOPE
Independent frozen lazy path/group helper and complete WelsMdPSkipEnc caller.
All QPs and pixel patterns, zero/overlapping/negative strides, 32/1984 stride benchmarks.
Score 5 versus 6 at QP12, crossing group boundaries, every group rejection, zero-max scan scratch preservation.
Every int16 FF/MF bit pattern; scalar DCT + historical SSE2 quantizer oracle; extreme QP/pixel cases.
Exact guard pages for pixels, 64 coefficients, 8-word tables and 4 maxima; complete 16x16 guarded caller.
Custom callback trace/replacement/state mutations, source/prediction/coefficient/level aliases, nil/short/capacity-only/extreme inputs and partial panic writes.
Zero lazy-caller allocations; complete-caller allocations compared with the independent previous implementation.

RISK / GATE
Reflect identity and whole-span preflight overhead may outweigh fusion on first-group rejects. Both are inside timed callers. Prototype duplicates DCT assembly arithmetic and skip score loop; parity tests freeze their independent previous behavior. Native/frame/streamhash/decoder gates remain required before selection.
